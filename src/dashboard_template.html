How it's calculated

Safety stock
z × √(LT·σd² + d²·σLT²)
Covers both demand and lead-time uncertainty. σd = forecast error.
Reorder point
d × LT + safety stock
Order when inventory position falls to this level.
Economic order quantity
√(2·D·S / H)
Balances ordering cost against holding cost.
Total landed cost
units × price × (1 + freight + duty + brokerage) + (SS + in-transit) × unit × holding %
Compares offshore and nearshore sources on total cost, not just price.
Forecast accuracy (WAPE)
Σ|actual − forecast| / Σ actual
Works when a week has zero sales, unlike MAPE.
